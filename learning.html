Electrical Basics

Electrical work samajhne ke liye sabse pehle basic electrical concepts ko samajhna zaroori hai.

Voltage: Electrical pressure ko voltage kehte hain. Unit Volt (V) hai.
Current: Circuit mein electrical charge ka flow current hai. Unit Ampere (A) hai.
Resistance: Current ke flow ko oppose karne ko resistance kehte hain. Unit Ohm (Ω) hai.
Power: Electrical equipment kitni power use karta hai, use Watt (W) mein measure kiya jata hai.
Energy: Electricity consumption ko commonly kWh, yaani "Unit", mein measure kiya jata hai.
AC & DC: AC ka direction time ke saath change hota hai, jabki DC ek direction mein flow karta hai.
